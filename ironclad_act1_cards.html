Ironclad — Act 1 card effectiveness (A9, A10)

Cohort = Ironclad runs (A9, A10) that reached at least floor 8. For each card, we compare the Act 2 clear rate of runs that had picked it up by that floor against those that hadn't. The Winrate column is the full-run (all 3 acts) win rate among runs that picked up the card.

Correlational, not causal: card picks are the player's choice, not a random draw. A stronger player tends to both pick better and survive more. Campfire Smith upgrades («Smith: Name») aren't tied to the card's id, so they aren't counted here. Rows marked * have a small sample (n < 40): the gap may just be noise.
