n (with) = observations (one per boss fought) where the run owned that card by the time of the fight. Avg HP lost = floors.damage_taken for that fight, averaged. Diff (HP saved) = avg without minus avg with, combined across the 4 possible Act 3 bosses with a weighted stratified average — positive means the card correlates with less damage taken. Rows marked * have min(n_with, n_without) < 100.
