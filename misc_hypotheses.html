Miscellaneous hypotheses

Small, one-off questions that don't warrant their own dedicated report. Each is scored as a single number across all 5 characters combined (rather than broken out per character like the other reports on this site).

Fast facts

  • Shopping before floor 7 helps overall (+2.4% Act 1 clear) — and much more with a Golden Pearl start (+7.4%) than without (+2.1%).
  • Fighting an elite before floor 7 hurts on average (−6.6% Act 1 clear) — true for every Neow relic with a decent sample; no relic makes it clearly worth it.
  • Fighting elites late in Act 1 (floor 9-16) helps a lot, and monotonically: 0→3+ elites is +15.5% Act 1 clear, +30.6% winrate.
  • More events than monster fights in floor 1-7 helps, but only up to a point — 0→2 events is +6.2% Act 1 clear, then it plateaus (no further gain past 2).
  • At a campfire, Smith beats Heal on average whether you're above 50% HP (+11.6% clear) or at/below it (+7.7% clear) — no HP threshold where Heal wins. Likely reflects who chooses to skip healing, not that healing itself hurts.
  • Act 2 only half-mirrors Act 1: fighting an elite early in Act 2 is roughly neutral (+0.6% clear, +4.4% winrate) — not bad the way early Act 1 elites are.
  • ...but late Act 2 elites help even more than late Act 1 elites: 0→3+ elites (floor 8 of the act to the boss) is +20.1% Act 2 clear, +34.5% winrate.
  • Buy-a-card vs remove-a-card in shops: not testable — card removal isn't captured anywhere in the scraped data (no reward marker for it, unlike card/relic/potion purchases).

Sample size note: most Golden Pearl starts already shop early (the extra gold makes the detour worth it), so the "did not shop" group within that stratum is smaller than in the other one — check the n before reading too much into it.

"Event" here means room_type unknown in the scraped data — confirmed by spot-checking: these rows carry event names (e.g. "Trash Heap", "Sunken Statue", "Brain Leech") in the encounter field, distinct from shops, treasure rooms, and monster/elite fights, which already have their own room_type. Read this as "more events, whatever they displace" rather than a strict one-for-one events-vs-monsters swap.

One observation per campfire decision, not per run — a run visits several campfires across a run, each an independent fresh choice (unlike the card upgrade value report's Smith question, there's no "first opportunity only" restriction needed here, since choosing Heal doesn't remove anything from a future campfire's eligible choices the way Smithing a card does). HP is measured at arrival, before the choice (the previous floor's HP/max HP), not after — otherwise choosing Heal would trivially show "high HP after" by construction.

"Cleared the act" = the run went on to reach a later act than the one this campfire happened in — the same idea as "Act 1 clear rate" elsewhere on this page, generalized since campfires happen in every act, not just Act 1. Winrate = won the whole run.

Campfire choices other than Smith/Heal (rare relic-granted alternates: Hatch, Dig, Lift) are excluded — this hypothesis is specifically about the Smith-vs-Heal tradeoff.
