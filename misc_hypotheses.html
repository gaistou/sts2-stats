Miscellaneous hypotheses

Small, one-off questions that don't warrant their own dedicated report. Most are scored as a single number across all 5 characters combined (rather than broken out per character like the other reports on this site) — a few cards break the same question down by character instead, where the point is comparing classes against each other.

Most events can't be split by the choice made inside them — the scraped data only has the event's name and what came out of it, not the literal choice text. No landmark-floor restriction: this compares two branches of the same single decision point, once a run has already reached the event — every run that ran into it at all is one observation.

All 5 characters combined. Shown at both ascension 9-10 and pooled across every ascension, side by side, rather than picking one — the two roughly agree in direction but not magnitude.
