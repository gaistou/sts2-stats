Miscellaneous hypotheses

Small, one-off questions that don't warrant their own dedicated report. Most are scored as a single number across all 5 characters combined (rather than broken out per character like the other reports on this site) — a few cards break the same question down by character instead, where the point is comparing classes against each other.
