Event choices

For the handful of Act 1 events where the two branches leave a distinguishable signal in the data (damage taken, or an HP change), how does picking one over the other correlate with Act 1 clear rate and full-run winrate? Most events can't be split this way — the raw data only has the event's name and what came out of it, not the literal choice text — so this page covers only the events confirmed to have a reliable per-branch signal.
