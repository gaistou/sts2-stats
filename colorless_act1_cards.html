Colorless cards — Act 1 card effectiveness, all classes (all ascensions)

All 5 characters pooled (Colorless cards can be picked up by any class). N = times picked up by floor 9. With / Without = Act 1 clear rate (reached Act 2) for runs that had the card by then vs runs that didn't; Diff = With minus Without, combined across characters weighted to avoid a Simpson's-paradox confound (see methodology). Δ (A9/A10 only) re-runs the same comparison restricted to ascension 9-10 — dash = no A9/A10 data, blank = below the min-sample filter. Global winrate (all 3 acts) is a separate, unrestricted stat — see methodology. Rows marked * have a small sample (n < 100) — expect several here, since Colorless cards (especially Ancient rarity) are picked up far less often than a class's own cards.

Full methodology, bias correction, and caveats: see the Act 1 card effectiveness methodology on the homepage.
